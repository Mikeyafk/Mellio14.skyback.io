Level 0-250 Progression

Players in this level range have many different options for improving their profile.

Find Your Two Weakest Areas

For players between SkyBlock Level 0 and 250, there is not always one specific activity that needs to be completed next.

A useful approach is to compare Skills, Dungeons, Slayers, and Gear and focus on two areas that are behind the rest of your profile.

For example, a player with strong skills but a low Catacombs level might benefit more from Dungeons than continuing to grind their highest skills.

If Dungeons Is Behind

Dungeons unlock stronger armor, weapons, and other progression opportunities.

RECOMMENDED FOCUS

Increase Your Catacombs Level

If Dungeons is one of your weakest areas, start by increasing your Catacombs level and unlocking additional Dungeon floors.

One possible progression path is obtaining a Floor 7 completion, then working toward Necron Armor with a Golden Head for the floor you are running.

As your Catacombs level increases, you can begin investing in stronger weapons and pets.

Long-Term Goals

  • Increase your Catacombs level
  • Unlock Floor 7
  • Work toward Necron Armor
  • Use the appropriate Golden Head
  • Work toward an Astrea or Hyperion
  • Work toward a Terminator
  • Consider a Baby Yeti Pet

Consider Playing Tank

Tank can be a good class to begin investing in because it can provide value to a Dungeon party without requiring as expensive a setup as some other classes.

This can give players a way to gain Catacombs experience while continuing to improve their equipment.
