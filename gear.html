Understanding Gear Progression

General equipment recommendations for your profile.

Choosing the Right Equipment

A lot of the best general combat equipment comes from Dungeons, especially as players begin moving beyond their earliest setups.

This guide focuses on useful general equipment rather than setups designed for one activity. The goal is to show which weapons, armor, and pets you can work toward as you progress.

For specialized Mining, Fishing, Farming, and Foraging setups, visit the Skills page.

GEAR PROGRESSION

Weapon Progression

Upgrade your weapons as you move into more difficult combat content.

EARLY GAME

Void Sword

An affordable starting weapon for early combat progression.

How to Obtain

Purchase from an NPC in The End for 200,000 coins.

MID GAME

Aspect of the Dragons

A strong upgrade from the Void Sword that can help you progress toward stronger Dungeon equipment.

How to Obtain

Obtain from dragons in The End or purchase from the Auction House.

LATE GAME

Shadow Fury / Flower of Truth

Useful Dungeon-related weapon upgrades. Shadow Fury provides strong melee damage, while Flower of Truth offers another combat option.

How to Obtain

Associated with Catacombs Floors 5 and 6. They can also be purchased from players.

END GAME

Hyperion / Terminator

Two major high-end weapons. Hyperion provides powerful ability damage, while Terminator is excellent for advanced ranged combat.

How to Obtain

Hyperion comes from Floor 7 progression. Terminator comes from Tier 4 Enderman Slayer progression. Both can also be purchased through the Auction House.

GEAR PROGRESSION

Armor Progression

Improve your damage and survivability with stronger armor sets.

EARLY GAME

Ender Armor

An accessible starting set that helps with combat in The End.

How to Obtain

Obtain from Endermen in The End.

MID GAME

Superior Dragon Armor

A well-rounded armor set that improves general combat stats.

How to Obtain

Obtain from Superior Dragons in The End or purchase from other players.

LATE GAME

Necron Armor

A powerful damage-focused armor set for higher-level Dungeon progression.

How to Obtain

Obtain through Floor 7 Dungeon progression.

END GAME

Infernal Crimson Armor

A high-end combat armor goal for players focusing on difficult content outside Dungeons.

How to Obtain

Progress through Kuudra and upgrade Crimson Armor to Infernal tier.

GEAR PROGRESSION

Pet Progression

Pets provide valuable bonuses to improve combat performance.

EARLY GAME

Enderman Pet

An accessible starting pet for early combat progression, especially in The End.

How to Obtain

Obtain as a drop from Endermen in The End.

MID GAME

Tiger Pet

A useful damage-focused pet for general combat activities.

How to Obtain

Purchase from Oringo during the Traveling Zoo event in the Hub.

LATE GAME

Ender Dragon Pet

A powerful pet that improves overall combat stats. Buying one is often more practical than trying to obtain the rare drop.

How to Obtain

Rarely obtained from dragons in The End or purchased from the Auction House.

END GAME

Golden Dragon Pet

One of the strongest and most expensive pets to work toward. Best suited to players with powerful equipment and significant funds.

How to Obtain

Obtain from the Dragon's Lair in the Crystal Hollows.

Keep Improving Your Profile

Strong equipment is only one part of progression.

Gear Is Only Part of the Game

Expensive weapons, armor, and pets do not automatically mean your profile is well progressed. Skills, Catacombs, Slayers, and other areas are also important.
