SKYBLOCK SKILL GUIDES

Improve Your Skills

Skills are an important part of Hypixel SkyBlock progression. Explore different leveling methods, equipment, and goals to help improve your profile.

Before You Start

Keep these tips in mind when working on your skills.

Use the Right Equipment

Specialized tools, armor, and pets can make certain skills much easier to level.

Take Advantage of Bonuses

Skill XP boosts, temporary events, and useful pet bonuses can improve your efficiency.

Focus on Weaker Skills

Improving your lowest skills is often a useful way to raise your overall skill average.

SKILL 01

Combat

Combat XP comes from defeating enemies. Stronger enemies and specialized activities can provide better progression opportunities.

EARLY GAME

Build Your Combat Level

Begin by defeating enemies in the Deep Caverns, Spider's Den, and The End.

Recommended Focus

  • Improve your weapon damage.
  • Use affordable combat armor.
  • Defeat Endermen when you can survive them.
  • Start working on basic Slayer quests.

MID GAME

Expand Your Combat Options

Continue progressing through Slayers, Dungeons, and stronger combat areas.

Recommended Focus

  • Improve your damage and survivability.
  • Progress through Dungeon floors.
  • Complete higher Slayer tiers.
  • Use a combat-focused pet when useful.

LATE GAME

Higher-Level Combat

Higher-tier Slayer bosses, Dungeons, and advanced combat content become more practical.

Recommended Focus

  • Upgrade weapons and armor.
  • Work on difficult Slayer bosses.
  • Improve Magical Power.
  • Use appropriate pets and equipment.

END GAME

Optimize Your Combat Setup

Focus on difficult combat activities while improving efficiency and consistency.

Recommended Focus

  • Complete advanced Slayer content.
  • Optimize high-level Dungeon setups.
  • Improve damage-related stats.
  • Work toward maximum Combat level.

SKILL 02

Mining

Mining progression involves improving your tools, Heart of the Mountain, Mining Speed, and Mining Fortune.

EARLY GAME

Start Mining

Mine ores in the Gold Mine and Deep Caverns before progressing into the Dwarven Mines.

Recommended Focus

  • Upgrade your pickaxe.
  • Unlock the Dwarven Mines.
  • Complete daily commissions.
  • Begin Heart of the Mountain progression.

MID GAME

Improve Your Mining Setup

Progress through the Dwarven Mines and Crystal Hollows.

Recommended Focus

  • Continue completing commissions.
  • Unlock additional Heart of the Mountain perks.
  • Upgrade mining equipment.
  • Begin collecting Gemstone Powder.

LATE GAME

Powder and Gemstone Progression

Improve your Mining Speed and Mining Fortune through stronger equipment and powder upgrades.

Recommended Focus

  • Upgrade your drill or mining tool.
  • Improve your gemstone mining setup.
  • Invest powder into useful perks.
  • Use a mining-focused pet.

END GAME

Efficient Mining

Use an optimized mining setup to improve both experience gain and resource collection.

Recommended Focus

  • Optimize high-tier mining equipment.
  • Improve gemstone upgrades.
  • Maximize useful mining perks.
  • Work toward maximum Mining level.

SKILL 03

Fishing

Fishing progression involves catching fish, defeating Sea Creatures, and upgrading fishing equipment.

EARLY GAME

Begin Fishing

Start with basic fishing rods and accessible fishing locations.

Recommended Focus

  • Upgrade your fishing rod.
  • Improve Sea Creature Chance.
  • Use fishing-related enchantments.
  • Unlock better fishing equipment.

MID GAME

Sea Creature Fishing

Focus on catching and defeating Sea Creatures for Fishing experience.

Recommended Focus

  • Upgrade fishing armor.
  • Improve Fishing Speed.
  • Use a fishing pet when useful.
  • Participate in fishing events.

LATE GAME

Lava Fishing

Crimson Isle fishing introduces additional Sea Creatures and equipment progression.

Recommended Focus

  • Unlock suitable lava fishing equipment.
  • Improve Fishing Speed and Sea Creature Chance.
  • Upgrade specialized fishing armor.
  • Explore higher-level fishing content.

END GAME

Advanced Fishing

Focus on efficient fishing methods, difficult Sea Creatures, and optimized gear.

Recommended Focus

  • Optimize your fishing setup.
  • Use advanced fishing equipment.
  • Improve your fishing-related stats.
  • Work toward maximum Fishing level.

SKILL 04

Foraging

Foraging progression focuses on collecting wood and improving the equipment used to gather it.

EARLY GAME

Collect Wood

Start by chopping trees and unlocking better axes and Foraging areas.

Recommended Focus

  • Use an axe suited to your level.
  • Explore accessible wood types.
  • Improve your collection efficiency.

MID GAME

Upgrade Your Equipment

Continue improving your Foraging equipment and collecting resources.

Recommended Focus

  • Upgrade your axe.
  • Use useful equipment bonuses.
  • Focus on consistent wood collection.

LATE GAME

Improve Efficiency

Work on more efficient collection methods and advanced Foraging progression.

Recommended Focus

  • Improve collection-related stats.
  • Use specialized Foraging equipment.
  • Explore higher-level Foraging content.

END GAME

Optimize Foraging

Continue upgrading equipment and work toward your long-term Foraging goals.

Recommended Focus

  • Optimize your Foraging setup.
  • Improve gathering efficiency.
  • Work toward maximum Foraging level.

SKILL 05

Farming

Farming is a popular progression skill because the Garden provides a dedicated area for growing and harvesting crops.

EARLY GAME

Start in the Garden

Unlock the Garden and begin growing accessible crops.

Recommended Focus

  • Unlock additional Garden plots.
  • Build simple crop farms.
  • Use basic farming tools.
  • Increase your Farming level.

MID GAME

Improve Farming Fortune

Upgrade your equipment and begin focusing on more efficient harvesting.

Recommended Focus

  • Use crop-specific farming tools.
  • Upgrade farming armor.
  • Improve Farming Fortune.
  • Participate in Jacob's Farming Contests.

LATE GAME

Advanced Garden Farming

Continue improving your farming setup and crop collection efficiency.

Recommended Focus

  • Upgrade specialized farming tools.
  • Improve armor and equipment bonuses.
  • Use a farming-focused pet.
  • Progress through Garden milestones.

END GAME

Optimize Your Farm

Focus on maximizing crop collection and improving experience efficiency.

Recommended Focus

  • Optimize Farming Fortune.
  • Improve crop-specific setups.
  • Complete remaining farming milestones.
  • Work toward maximum Farming level.

SKILL 06

Alchemy

Alchemy is primarily leveled by brewing potions. Planning your brewing materials can make the process more convenient.

Brewing for Alchemy Experience

Use Brewing Stands to create potions. Different brewing ingredients can provide different amounts of Alchemy experience.

Plan your ingredients before beginning a large brewing session. Some methods can become expensive, so consider the cost of the materials.

You can also look for temporary skill experience bonuses before leveling Alchemy.

SKILL 07

Enchanting

Enchanting is an important skill for improving equipment and unlocking stronger enchantments.

Use the Experimentation Table

The Experimentation Table provides a useful way to earn Enchanting experience through experiments.

Complete your available experiments regularly. As your Enchanting level increases, you can unlock additional progression opportunities.

A Guardian pet can also be useful for Enchanting-related progression.

SKILL 08

Taming

Taming progression is connected to earning experience with your pets.

Level Your Pets

Keep an appropriate pet equipped while completing activities that provide skill experience.

As your pets gain experience, you can also make progress toward your Taming goals.

Consider which pets match the activities you are currently doing, such as Mining, Farming, or Combat.

SKILL 09

Hunting

Hunting is a specialized SkyBlock skill with its own progression activities, equipment, and collection goals.

Explore Hunting Progression

Begin by exploring Hunting content and learning how its mechanics work.

As you progress, improve the equipment and bonuses associated with Hunting to make your activities more efficient.

Work through available Hunting goals while continuing to improve your overall profile.
