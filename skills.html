SKYBLOCK SKILLS

Skill Progression Guide

Skills are a major part of your SkyBlock profile. This guide gives you a progression path for each skill and shows what you can work toward as your profile improves.

Before You Start Grinding

There are several ways to make almost every skill grind faster before you even begin.

Mayors & Events

Take advantage of mayors and events that increase Skill XP. Derpy is especially useful because of his Skill XP boost.

Anniversary events also usually include some form of global Skill XP boost, making them a good time to work on skills you have been putting off.

Use Minions

Minions can provide passive Skill XP while you are doing other things.

  • Melon Minions - Farming XP
  • Tarantula Minions - Combat XP
  • Revenant Minions - Combat XP
  • Fishing Minions - Fishing XP
  • Snow Minions - Mining XP

XP Boosts

Having a God Potion active is very important when working on skills. You should also have a Booster Cookie buff whenever possible.

As you progress, unlock shards that provide additional Skill XP increases. Small XP bonuses become much more valuable during long grinds.

SKILL GUIDE

Combat

Combat is one of the most important skills to progress because it connects with Dungeons, Slayers, Bestiary, and many other parts of SkyBlock.

EARLY GAME

Ghosts

A strong early Combat XP method is killing Ghosts. Your goal is to build your Combat level while preparing for stronger Combat methods.

Recommended Setup

  • Mastiff Armor
  • Pooch Sword
  • Defensive Pet or Wolf Pet
  • God Potion
  • Booster Cookie

MID GAME

Floor 6 / Master Mode 6

Once you have a decent Dungeon setup, move toward Floor 6 or Master Mode Floor 6 for Combat XP.

Focus on killing Terracottas and Golems. Your Dungeon setup should be strong enough to kill these enemies consistently without slowing down the run too much.

This also allows you to increase your Catacombs level while working on Combat.

LATE GAME

Prepare for Blazes

Late-game Combat progression should begin preparing you for efficiently killing Smoldering Tomb Blazes.

Continue improving your damage and work toward the equipment needed for the end-game Combat method.

END GAME

Smoldering Tomb Blazes

One of the best overall Combat XP methods is killing Smoldering Tomb Blazes.

Recommended Setup

  • Full Aurora Armor
  • Hyperion
  • Golden Dragon

Your goal is to reach enough damage to consistently one-shot the Blazes. The faster you can kill them, the more effective the method becomes.

SKILL GUIDE

Mining

Mining progression is heavily connected to commissions, Heart of the Mountain progression, and gradually upgrading your Mining equipment.

EARLY GAME

Start Commissions

Begin completing commissions and start progressing through Heart of the Mountain.

Recommended Setup

  • Glacite Armor
  • Mithril Pickaxe from the NPC

You do not need an expensive setup yet. Your main goal is completing commissions and unlocking more Mining progression.

MID GAME

Continue Commissions

Keep completing commissions while beginning to invest more into your Mining equipment.

Recommended Setup

  • Sorrow or Mineral Armor
  • Titanium Drill DR-X355

LATE GAME

Divan Progression

Continue commissions and begin moving into your stronger Mining equipment.

Recommended Setup

  • Clean Divan's Armor
  • Titanium Drill DR-X555
  • Around Heart of the Mountain 7

END GAME

Max Mining Setup

Mining should normally already be maxed by this point. If you have the coins to purchase the equipment outright, you can work toward a maximum Mining setup.

Recommended Setup

  • Maxed Divan's Armor with Coating
  • Divan's Drill
  • Pendant of Divan
  • Heart of the Mountain 10

Want More Mining Information?

Mining has many more mechanics than can be covered in a basic progression guide. Mining Cult has an extremely detailed guide for players who want to learn more about Mining.

SKILL GUIDE

Fishing

Fishing begins with inexpensive equipment but eventually progresses into much stronger specialized armor, rods, and pets.

EARLY GAME

Starting Fishing Setup

Start with an inexpensive setup that gives you everything you need to begin leveling Fishing.

Recommended Setup

  • Water Hydra Head
  • 2/4 Angler Armor
  • Squid Boots
  • Speedster Rod
  • Squid Pet

MID GAME

Upgrade Your Fishing Gear

Once you have progressed beyond your starting equipment, move into a stronger full armor setup and upgrade your fishing rod.

Recommended Setup

  • Full Diver's Armor or Sponge Armor
  • Rod of Legends

LATE GAME

Thunder Setup

At this stage, move into stronger Fishing gear that prepares you for end-game Fishing.

Recommended Setup

  • Thunder Armor
  • Inferno Rod

END GAME

Magma Lord Setup

Your end-game Fishing goal is moving into high-end Fishing armor and a stronger rod.

Recommended Setup

  • Magma Lord Armor
  • Hellfire Rod

SKILL GUIDE

Foraging

Foraging is an extremely early-game-friendly skill. You can begin progressing it without needing a large amount of coins or advanced equipment.

EARLY GAME

Start Foraging

Begin with an inexpensive setup and start building your Foraging level.

Recommended Setup

  • Spruce Axe
  • Park Armor
  • Ocelot Pet

MID GAME

Treecapitator Setup

Upgrade your equipment as you move beyond the earliest stages of Foraging.

Recommended Setup

  • Treecapitator
  • Fig Armor

LATE GAME

Figstone Splitter

Continue upgrading into stronger specialized Foraging equipment.

Recommended Setup

  • Figstone Splitter

END GAME

Helix Setup

Your final equipment goal is moving into the end-game Foraging setup.

Recommended Setup

  • Helix Armor
  • Helix Chopper

SKILL GUIDE

Farming

Farming progression is based around improving your armor, crop-specific tools, and gradually expanding into more crops.

EARLY GAME

Focus One Crop

Pick one crop and focus on it instead of trying to progress every crop immediately.

Recommended Setup

  • Farmhand Armor
  • Tier 1 Hoe for your chosen crop
  • Rabbit Pet

Build your Farming level and begin upgrading the tool for the crop you selected.

MID GAME

Expand Into More Crops

Once you have progressed your first crop, begin expanding into the other crops and collecting their tools.

Recommended Setup

  • Cropie Armor
  • Obtain hoes for all crops
  • Upgrade some hoes to Tier 2

LATE GAME

Fermento Progression

At this point, focus on finishing the major upgrades for your Farming setup.

Recommended Setup

  • Fermento Armor
  • Tier 3 all crop hoes

END GAME

Max Farming Equipment

End-game Farming becomes less about unlocking basic equipment and more about maximizing your equipment for every crop.

Recommended Setup

  • Max all crop hoes
  • Helianthus Armor

SKILL GUIDE

Alchemy

Alchemy does not have the same Early, Mid, Late, and End Game progression as most other skills.

Wait for Derpy

The main strategy for Alchemy is to take advantage of Derpy when he is available.

Prepare the coins you need ahead of time and purchase Enchanted Sugar Cane. When Derpy is active, use the Enchanted Sugar Cane for brewing to quickly gain Alchemy XP.

What You Need

  • Enchanted Sugar Cane
  • Parrot Pet
  • God Potion
  • Booster Cookie

Because Alchemy can be progressed quickly when the correct bonuses are active, it usually makes more sense to prepare for Derpy instead of slowly grinding the skill throughout your profile.

SKILL GUIDE

Enchanting

Enchanting does not require a traditional equipment progression path.

Complete Your Daily Minigame

The main way to progress Enchanting is to consistently complete your daily Enchanting minigame.

Because Enchanting is heavily based around its daily activity, consistency is more important than buying several different equipment setups.

Try not to ignore your daily Enchanting XP while working on your other skills. Completing it regularly allows your Enchanting level to increase alongside the rest of your profile.

SKILL GUIDE

Taming

Taming is mainly a passive skill that you can progress while working on your other skills.

Level Pets While Grinding

Taming increases as you gain experience with pets. Because of this, you normally do not need to stop progressing another skill specifically to grind Taming.

While leveling another skill, use EXP Share on pets that benefit from the type of Skill XP you are earning. This lets you progress additional pets while continuing your normal skill grind.

Farming Example

If you are working on Farming, you can EXP Share Farming-related pets such as:

  • Elephant
  • Mooshroom Cow
  • Rose Dragon

The same idea can be used while leveling other skills. Try to level useful pets whenever you are already completing a long skill grind.

SKILL GUIDE

Hunting

Hunting does not follow the same traditional gear progression as skills such as Mining or Fishing.

Progress Hunting While Foraging

Hunting can be progressed alongside Foraging instead of treating the two skills as completely separate grinds.

While leveling Foraging on the Foraging Island, participate in Miria Contests to earn tickets.

Use those tickets to enter the Safari. While participating in the Safari, you can gain Hunting XP and continue progressing the skill.

Basic Progression

  1. Work on your Foraging level.
  2. Participate in Miria Contests.
  3. Earn tickets from the contests.
  4. Use the tickets to enter the Safari.
  5. Gain Hunting XP through the Safari.
